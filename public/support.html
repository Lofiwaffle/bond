Help & support

Bond is a two-minute daily check-in for two people. It is not therapy, counseling, medical care, or emergency support. If you are in danger, contact local emergency services.

In the app

Open Us for your account, pairing, export, and Delete account. Open Help & safety for crisis resources that do not notify a partner.

Report a problem

If something in Bond is harmful, abusive, or should not be there, email the support address on the Play Store or App Store listing. Include the date if you can. We cannot read private thoughts stored only on a device.

You can also leave a Bond (Us → Leave this Bond) or delete your account (Us → Delete account). There is no public feed.

Request account and data deletion

To delete your account and its associated data immediately, open Bond and choose Us → Delete account. If you cannot access the app, email a deletion request using the support address on the App Store or Play Store listing. Include the email address used for your Bond account so we can identify it.
